The Deep Dive: Why the Pace Itself Is the Point

The Simple Version

AI is not just getting better. It's getting better faster than anything we've seen in modern business history. And the speed of change, not any individual tool or model, is now the thing you need to build your strategy around.

The Context

In 2020, there were 11 notable AI models released globally. In 2024, that number was 81. And in 2025, we're tracking meaningful new model releases every single week.

In January 2025, DeepSeek released a model that matched leading American AI systems at a reported training cost of approximately $6 million, versus comparable American models costing upwards of $100 million. Nvidia's stock dropped $589 billion in a single trading day — the largest single-day market cap loss in history.

This is what I mean when I say the pace itself is the point. We're living through a compounding acceleration where each breakthrough reduces the cost and time required for the next one.

The Analogy

In 2020, AI was dial-up. By 2023, broadband. In 2025, we have fiber optic AI. The infrastructure constraint is effectively gone. The question is no longer "is this good enough to be useful?" It's "what are we going to build now that the infrastructure supports it?"

You couldn't run Netflix on dial-up. That infrastructure constraint is now solved for AI — the question has shifted to: what business model do you want to build on top of it?

Monks Success Story

In January 2024, at CES in Las Vegas, we unveiled Monks.Flow — an AI-native marketing platform. Eighteen months later, at Cannes Lions 2025, we presented it as a fully operational "agent marketing factory" running at scale for some of the most demanding brands in the world.

In those same 18 months, Monks was named Adweek's first-ever AI Agency of the Year. Not because we had impressive slides. Because we had the most sophisticated deployment.

The pace of AI means the advantage goes to organizations that accept the discomfort of building before everything is certain.

"You don't need to understand every AI model. You need to understand that the tools your competitors will use in six months are already available today, and they're getting better every week."

Your One Action This Week

Pick one real work task and do it using ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini instead of starting from scratch. Not a toy task. A real deliverable you would normally produce yourself.

The goal is calibration — a current, first-hand understanding of where AI actually is in its capabilities.

Start there. One task. This week.
